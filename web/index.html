Browser Togyzkumalak engine

Choose players, set bot time per move, optionally paste a FEN, then start. Human players have no timer.

  1. Click one of your nine pits to move.
  2. Bots run locally in WebAssembly inside your browser.
  3. Download the move list as 9Q-PGN from the game page.

Formats

9Q-FEN

FEN has five fields: white pits / black pits, kazans, tuzdyks, side to move, and half-move count.

9,9,9,9,9,9,9,9,9/9,9,9,9,9,9,9,9,9 0,0 -,- w 0

Pits are listed 1-9 for each owner. Tuzdyks use owner pit numbers or “-”. Side is “w” or “b”.

9Q-PGN

PGN stores chess-like tags followed by move pairs. Each move is two digits: origin pit and landing pit, both 1-9 relative to the owner of that cell.

1. 19 59 2. 22+ 72x

“+” marks a capture. “x” marks tuzdyk creation.
